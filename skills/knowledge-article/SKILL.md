# Knowledge article

Write the article with these sections: Title (symptom in the customer's words), Applies to (product and versions), Symptoms, Cause, Resolution (numbered steps), Verification, Related articles.

Remove customer names, hostnames, tokens and any other identifying data. Keep steps short and testable.
